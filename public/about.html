About InternHub

Make job and internship discovery simpler, clearer and more accessible for students and early-career professionals.

Our Mission

We built InternHub because we believe every student deserves a fair chance at finding the right opportunity. The traditional process is broken, opaque, and frustrating. We're here to change that.

Our Vision

To become the default platform for early-career opportunities, connecting millions of students with thousands of forward-thinking companies globally.
